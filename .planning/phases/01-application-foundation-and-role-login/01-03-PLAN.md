<threat_model>
| Threat | STRIDE | Disposition | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Browser-side role spoofing | Elevation of privilege | mitigate | UI role checks are presentation only; API guards from Plan 02 remain authoritative. |
| Session cookie omitted from frontend requests | Spoofing | mitigate | Centralize auth fetch helpers with `credentials: 'include'`. |
| Confusing auth failure messages | Repudiation | mitigate | Display clear login/register errors while avoiding account enumeration details from the API. |
</threat_model>
